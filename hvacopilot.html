Frequently asked questions

Know what is—and isn't—included.

Are voice, SMS, and software usage included in the monthly fee?

No. The $99 or $249 fee is the HVACopilot agency fee. Retell AI usage, Twilio SMS and phone number, n8n, and any required paid Airtable seats or other provider plans are paid directly by your business.

Do you provide a complete field-service management platform?

No. HVACopilot v1 focuses on AI call handling, booking, notifications, CRM records, and—on Growth—technician assignment and job tracking. Route planning, GPS tracking, estimates, inventory, payroll, invoicing, payment processing, custom mobile apps, and large CRM migrations are outside the standard scope.

Will the AI confirm an appointment before it is booked?

No. The workflow is designed to confirm the booking only after Google Calendar successfully creates the event. If the slot is unavailable or booking fails, it should offer another time or alert the owner rather than invent a confirmation.

Who owns the accounts and business data?

Your business owns the production accounts, phone number, calendar, and data. Provider credentials are kept in the automation platform's credential store, not in customer records.

Can the system handle urgent or out-of-scope calls?

We configure routing and escalation rules with you during onboarding. The agreed flow can transfer the call to a human and alert the owner when a call needs human attention.

Is custom development included?

Custom integrations and major development are charged separately. Growth includes up to two agreed minor improvements per month; exact support scope and response targets are confirmed in the service agreement.
